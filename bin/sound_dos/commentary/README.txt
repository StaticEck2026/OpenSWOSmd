Your own commentary
===================

Make a folder here for any of the categories below and put sound files in it: .wav, .mp3
or .raw (the PC game's own format: unsigned 8-bit mono, 22,050 Hz). Any number of files,
of any length. The names are SWOS 2020's, so a commentary pack made for it can be copied
in as it is.

A category with files in its folder is spoken from them, picked at random and never the
same one twice running. A category without a folder, or with an empty one, keeps the PC
game's own lines.

A file whose name ends in _x3 or -x3 (before the extension) is three times as likely to
be picked as a plain one; any number works.

To keep a pack somewhere else, set commentary_dir under [audio] in SWOS.ini.

Commentary is heard with  style = pc  or  commentary = true  under [audio].

Categories
----------
corner                  a corner is given
throw_in                a throw-in is given
free_kick               a foul, outside the penalty area
dirty_tackle            a hard tackle that brings a player down without a foul
penalty                 a penalty is given
penalty_scored          the penalty goes in
penalty_missed          the penalty misses
penalty_saved           the penalty is saved
goal                    a goal
own_goal                an own goal
near_miss               a shot just wide or over
hit_post                the ball hits a post
hit_bar                 the ball hits the bar
keeper_saved            the goalkeeper saves a shot
keeper_claimed          the goalkeeper gathers the ball
good_tackle             a clean tackle
good_play               every fifth pass to a team mate
yellow_card             a booking
red_card                a sending-off
substitution            a substitution
tactic_changed          a change of tactics
end_game_rout           full time, won by three goals or more
end_game_sensational    full time, four goals or more in the match
end_game_so_close       full time, a draw           (no line in the PC game)
header                  a header by your own player (no line in the PC game)
injury                  your own player is injured  (no line in the PC game)
